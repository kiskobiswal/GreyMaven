Strategy overview

The Steady Income Fund holds a laddered mix of government and investment-grade corporate bonds, structured to generate consistent income while limiting exposure to any single issuer or maturity date.

It's built for the portion of a portfolio meant to hold steady — a role usually reserved for institutional fixed-income desks or the most conservative retail money-market funds, with little in between.

Built for the account in between

Retail bond funds are often a single fund-of-funds with limited transparency into duration or credit risk. Institutional fixed-income mandates offer that transparency and active duration management, but at minimums far above what most individual accounts can meet. This fund brings that same discipline to a $10,000 minimum.

Fund-specific questions

How is income paid out?+
Interest income is distributed quarterly, or automatically reinvested if you prefer to compound.
What's the average bond duration?+
The fund targets an intermediate duration of roughly 5–7 years, balancing yield against interest-rate sensitivity.
Can I lose money in this fund?+
Bond values can fluctuate with interest rates and credit conditions, but this fund is built to be the lowest-volatility strategy we offer.
