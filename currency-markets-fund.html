Strategy overview

The Currency Markets Fund takes diversified positions across major global currency pairs, aiming to capture rate differentials and relative-value opportunities across economies.

Currency trading at this level of diversification and active management is typically the domain of institutional forex desks or macro hedge funds — rarely offered as a standalone, individually accessible fund.

Built for the account in between

Retail currency exposure usually means a leveraged spot forex account with high risk of rapid loss, or no exposure at all. Institutional macro desks manage currency risk in a diversified, disciplined way — but only for large allocators. This fund applies that same discipline starting at $15,000.

Fund-specific questions

Is this the same as retail forex trading?+
No. This fund is diversified across multiple pairs with position sizing designed to limit drawdown, unlike leveraged single-pair retail forex trading.
What drives returns in this fund?+
Interest-rate differentials between currencies and relative-value positioning based on macroeconomic conditions.
How correlated is this fund to the stock market?+
Currency markets historically show low correlation to equities, which is part of why clients use this fund for diversification.
