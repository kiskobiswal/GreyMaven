Strategy overview

The All-in-One Fund combines equities, bonds, and cash in a single, professionally managed allocation — built for investors who want a complete strategy without holding five different funds.

It's the lowest-minimum, most accessible entry point into GreyMaven's institutional-style approach, and the fund most new clients start with before adding satellite positions like our Digital Assets or Property Income funds.

Built for the account in between

Target-date and balanced retail funds exist, but are often generic and rarely actively adjusted. This fund is actively rebalanced with the same discipline as our single-asset-class strategies, just blended into one holding — at the lowest minimum we offer.
