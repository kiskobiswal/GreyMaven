Strategy overview

The Digital Assets Fund holds a managed basket of major cryptocurrencies through institutional custody, so you get direct market exposure without self-custody risk, exchange hacks, or wallet management.

This is a high-volatility strategy by design. It exists for clients who want the kind of risk institutional trading desks take on this asset class — not a token allocation, and not unmanaged speculation on a retail exchange app.

Built for the account in between

Retail investors are typically limited to buying crypto directly on an exchange, with no active management and full self-custody risk. Institutional desks run actively managed crypto books, but they're closed to individuals. This fund is actively managed and custodied the same way, opened at a $25,000 minimum instead of an institutional one.

Fund-specific questions

Why is there a 90-day lock-up?+
Digital asset markets can move sharply on short-term sentiment. The lock-up lets us manage the fund with a strategic horizon rather than reacting to daily withdrawal pressure.
How is custody handled?+
Assets are held with a regulated institutional digital asset custodian — you never manage a private key or wallet directly.
How much of my portfolio should be here?+
We generally discuss this fund as a smaller, higher-risk allocation alongside more conservative holdings — never as your only fund.
